Earn on your GEN

Supply GEN to a pool and earn 10% a year, counted per second. No lock-up — take it out whenever you like, interest included.

ℹ️ The yield is a subsidy, not revenue. It is paid from a reward balance the operator tops up — trading fees do not cover it. Each pool shows how long its reward balance lasts at the current size, and if it runs out your principal still comes back in full while the unpaid interest is recorded rather than forgotten.

How it works

1 — Supply
Send GEN to a pool. It starts earning the moment the transaction lands.
2 — Earn
Interest accrues every second at the advertised rate. Nothing to claim on a schedule, nothing to compound manually, no gas spent while it grows.
3 — Withdraw
Take out any amount at any time. Interest is settled in proportion to what you withdraw, so a partial exit leaves the rest earning.
What the two pools actually do
Predict is a pure yield position — players there win from each other, so your capital is never at risk from their trading. Perps is different: your capital backs trader profit and loss, so it can shrink if traders win, and withdrawals are limited to liquidity not currently backing an open position. That risk is what the yield on that side is compensating for.
